Admin Help



System Management Guide



  • Managing Drivers: Go to "Drivers Management" to add, edit, or delete drivers. Use the form to add a new driver or the buttons next to each driver to edit/delete.
  • Managing Users: Visit "Users Management" to add, edit, or delete users. Similar to drivers, use the form or buttons for actions.
  • Managing Requests: In the Dashboard, review "Pending Notifications," "Pending Purchase Requests," and "Pending Maintenance Requests." Click "Approve" or "Reject" to process each request.
  • Changing Your Password: Navigate to the "Settings" page from the sidebar. Under the "Change Password" section, enter your current password, then provide a new password and confirm it. Click "Save Changes" to update your password.
  • Updating Your Profile: Go to "Settings" to update personal details such as your email or username. Fill in the updated information in the form and click "Save Changes" to apply the updates.
  • Managing Notifications: Visit the "Notifications" page to view system messages, such as task updates or user reports. Mark messages as read or delete them as needed using the provided buttons.
  • Logging Out Safely: To log out, click the "Logout" option in the sidebar. This ensures your session is securely ended, protecting your account from unauthorized access.
  • Viewing Admin Activity Logs: In the "Settings" page, you can find a section for activity logs that track your actions, such as approvals or rejections, for auditing purposes.
